# Results

This directory contains the small, non-disclosive manuscript-facing outputs
that are useful to readers. Large bootstrap draw files, regenerable county
intermediates and temporary diagnostics are produced locally by the clean
pipeline and are not duplicated here unless they are needed for a published
table or figure.

## Headline case

The principal spatial case is the support-balanced rent-indexed reference
(`m = 6.7088`), 5-year contract, family-balanced across the three independent
POLYSYS allocation families.

For that case:

- median biomass mobilization is **78.46%** of the upstream POLYSYS allocation;
- the paired respondent-bootstrap 95% interval is **70.34% to 86.08%**;
- the implemented structural range is **63.47% to 83.81%**;
- the three independent POLYSYS-family medians span **76.17% to 81.54%**;
- the 10-year contract gives **76.62%**, compared with **78.46%** for 5 years.

These are separate uncertainty/sensitivity classes and are not combined into
one interval.

## Spatial concentration and evidence support

- The top decile of counties by unmet biomass accounts for **56.4%** of the
  national unmet total.
- Texas alone accounts for **55.18%** of unmet biomass in the reporting layer.
- Texas, Oklahoma and New Mexico together account for **77.43%**.
- At the support-balanced reference, **69.99%** of production exposure lies
  within the Study-A experimental offer range.
- **103 counties** have zero or undefined contractual-capacity pressure in the
  display layer; these account for only **2.14%** of national unmet biomass.
- The two species directly tested in Study A cover **47.3% to 66.5%** of
  POLYSYS production across the three independent allocation families.
